## Abstract

Particle counts depend strongly on where the sample is drawn. We compare four
sampling points on a 210 bar hydraulic test rig.

## 3. Recommended practice

The representative sample must be taken from the **return line before the filter**.
Sampling downstream of the filter under-reports the ISO 4406 code by up to
**two classes**, because the filter has already removed the particles of interest.

Sampling from the reservoir is discouraged: settled particles bias the count low.

## 4. Results

Across 60 paired samples the mean discrepancy between the two locations was
**1.8 ISO classes**.
