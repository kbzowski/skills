## Abstract

We monitored 14 industrial gearboxes over 18 months. Envelope analysis of the
acceleration signal detected outer-race defects on average **21 days** before the
first temperature alarm was raised by the plant SCADA system.

## 2. Method

Accelerometers were mounted radially on the bearing housing. Signals were sampled at
**25.6 kHz** and processed with a bandpass of 2-8 kHz prior to Hilbert enveloping.

## 3. Results

The mean lead time was 21 days (SD 6 days). Inner-race defects gave a shorter lead
time of 12 days. In 2 of the 14 units no defect developed during the observation
window.

We did not evaluate any industrial fieldbus protocol for data transport; all data
was collected over a dedicated wired link.
